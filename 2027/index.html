Why a plan

Knowing what to do and doing it are two different things.

Resolutions fade by February because they're goals without a map. Most of us already know a lot about eating well. What's missing is the order to do things in, a realistic timeline, and someone who tells you what a win looks like at each step.

That's what this session gives you. You leave with a written plan built for your body and your life, and your first step already chosen.

Your pace, your call

Will you walk or sprint this year?

Walking

Small, steady steps

One or two changes at a time. Slower, steadier results that fit a full life.

Sprinting

An all-in effort

More changes at once and more structure, for faster results when you have the bandwidth.

Both work. They just lead to different timelines. We'll be honest with you about what each one will produce.

Three questions we'll ask you.

  1. If 2027 goes the way you hope, what will be different by next December?

  2. When you get there, how do you want to feel?

There are no wrong answers. What you say becomes the heart of your plan, and the words we come back to when things get hard.

Questions

Good to know before you book.

Is it covered by insurance?

It's billed to your insurance like a regular nutrition visit. Most of our patients pay $0 because their plan covers nutrition counseling as a preventive benefit. We check your benefits before your visit, so you'll know ahead of time.

Who is this for?

Anyone ready to make 2027 different. Current patients, past patients coming back, and people meeting us for the first time are all welcome.

I haven't been in for a while. Do I need to catch up first?

No. We start from wherever you are right now. That's what the session is for.

Can I do this by video?

Yes. Telehealth sessions have the most openings, and you get the same full session, plan, and workbook as an in-person visit.

Do I need a referral?

Some plans require one. Add your doctor's name and fax number on your prep form and we'll request it for you.

When does my plan start?

You start your 14-Day Reset on a day you choose after your session. Your plan-start visit is in January or February.

What if my insurance changes on January 1?

Upload your new card on your prep form. We'll verify the new plan so there are no surprises in the new year.
